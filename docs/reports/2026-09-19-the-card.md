# The card on Home: what was measured before and after (V2, 19 Sep 2026)

The founder's line: the gift is filled in on one card, on the first page, with or without an account, and the
measure is "screens before the object exists: five today, one wanted". This is what was counted, how, and what is
not measured yet.

## Screens a funder passes before the gift exists

| | before (17 Sep assistant) | after (the card) |
|---|---|---|
| screens before any money is asked for | 5: who, what, the condition's detail, how much and how long, the check | **1**: Home, with the card on it |
| addresses in the journey | 8 (`?step=who,what,detail,amount,check,account,paying,done`) | 4 (`?step=pay,account,paying,done`), and Home |
| surfaces between opening Viky and a gift existing | 8 | 2: the card, then the screen that pays for it |
| review screen before paying | 1 | 0: the object was built in front of the person |

How it was counted, so it can be counted again: the old screens are the values of the `Step` type in the deleted
`app/components/FundGift.tsx` (read at `git show 8bf8350:app/components/FundGift.tsx`), each of which drew its own
shell and its own address. The new ones are the `Step` type of `app/components/PayGift.tsx` plus Home. A sheet is
not counted as a screen: it is a modal dialog over the card, and the card stays where it is behind it.

## What a person does, gesture by gesture, to a filled card

Measured on 19 Sep 2026 on a Pixel 7 sized screen (390 x 844), against the built site, with nothing signed in:

1. Press **For**, type two first names, Done.
2. Press **will**, choose a condition; the sheet becomes its own questions, with the day's bar already suggested;
   Done. The card now also shows "for 30 days", because the length comes from the condition rather than from a
   question, and the shape appears under the card, empty.
3. Press **worth**, type the amount on the keypad, Done.
4. Press **for**, choose one of the three lengths or type another, Done.
5. **Pay $30.00**.

Four sheets, five presses plus what is typed, and the card is readable at every moment in between. The same walk
reloaded the page after step 4 and the card came back filled, which is the draft of D74 doing what it already did
for a card payment.

## What is not measured

- **Seconds to the first gesture, and seconds to a made gift.** That is V5's line, and it needs somebody who is not
  us. Nothing here says the new shape is faster, only that it is shorter in screens.
- **Whether anybody understands it.** The first real gift made on the card by the founder, unaided, is the test the
  line is finished by; a test suite cannot answer it.
- **The Pay surface.** It is the old screen's money half, unchanged and alive at the same address, and the vision's
  sheet (V3) is the next line. So "one screen and one sheet" is true of filling the gift in, not yet of paying for
  it.

## Size, for what it is worth

The assistant was 1,550 lines in one file. What replaces it: the card and its four sheets, 689 lines across seven
files, plus 297 lines of rules and storage shared with the paying screen (`src/gift-draft.ts`, `src/card-draft.ts`),
and the money half at 815 lines. Nothing was deleted from `src/`: the register, the readings, the routes and the
contracts are untouched, which is the point of cutting at the screen.
